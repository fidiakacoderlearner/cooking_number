    How to play

    1. A customer orders a number. Cook it and serve it before they lose patience.
    2. Tap the customer you want to cook for. Their tiles load onto the stand.
    3. Tap number and operator tiles to build the dish. Drag works too.
    4. Order of operations
      • × and ÷ are worked out first: 2 + 3 × 4 = 14.
      • Powers and roots apply to the number right before them: 2 + 3² = 11, and 16 gives √4.
    5. When the plate shows their number, tap Serve. The finished value is always visible before you serve.
    6. Serve the wrong dish and the customer walks out. No points, no second chance.
    7. Score grows with speed: a customer served with patience left pays more.
